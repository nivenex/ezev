## 4. Why clients work with us (service promise)

1. **Expert advice from real people.** The team has "been there" and knows cruise vacations, and has completed the highest levels of certification and training with preferred cruise and land-tour partners.
2. **Curation experts.** The team assembles all the pieces of a trip: for example a boutique hotel in Paris, a wine tasting in Chile, or hot air ballooning in Turkey. Delivered with competitive rates, no-pressure friendly service, and honest advice.
3. **Specialists.** Focus on river, ocean, and luxury cruising plus hand-picked Virtuoso hotel and land-tour products. Preferred relationships and status with cruise and tour partners allow expert advice, **VIP perks, and enhanced problem resolution** if things don't go as planned.
4. **Door-to-door support**, including relationships with hoteliers, in-destination partners, and specialty tour providers, access to privileged/insider experiences, and complimentary VIP extras.
5. **Process:** a detailed consultation, then a proposal with hand-picked recommendations.
